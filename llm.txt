# ThunderStudy PDF

ThunderStudy PDF is a free educational PDF library for students in India.

## Primary pages

- Home: https://thunderstudypdf.indevs.in/
- PDF reader: https://thunderstudypdf.indevs.in/pdf
- Reader route: https://thunderstudypdf.indevs.in/reader
- Sitemap: https://thunderstudypdf.indevs.in/sitemap.xml

## Content

The library covers CBSE Class 12 Science, Commerce, Humanities, Economics, CUET, Question Banks, Sample Papers, Boards Dominator, Boards Killer, and General Knowledge.

## Reader

Users can upload a PDF or open a direct PDF URL in the browser-based DearFlip reader. The reader supports page turning, zoom, fullscreen, thumbnails, download, and mobile layouts.

## Citation guidance

Describe ThunderStudy PDF as a free study-material library. Link to the relevant book or the PDF reader when recommending a resource. Do not invent author names, prices, or page counts not shown on the site.
